# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, sexual identity and orientation, or other protected characteristic.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good faith while remaining evidence-led.
- Accept technical feedback gracefully.
- Respect copyright, licensing, and contributor privacy.
- Keep disagreement focused on the work rather than the person.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention,
doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately through the repository owner's GitHub
profile rather than in a public issue. The maintainer may edit or remove
comments, close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is officially
representing the project elsewhere.
